# Ember — Product

## What it is

Ember is a resurrected Rolodex for PhDs and early-career professionals. It's a spinning card file of academic and professional contacts that visibly ages when you lose touch and helps you reach back out.

**Tagline:** "Every connection starts with a spark. Ember keeps it lit."

**Subhead:** "For PhDs, postdocs and early-career professionals whose careers run on connections."

## Who it's for

Primary users are people whose careers are built on networks:

- **PhD students** — especially those heading toward the job market
- **Research master's students** — navigating academic pipelines
- **Postdocs** — managing advisor and hiring relationships across institutions
- **Early-career faculty and industry-bound PhDs** — maintaining dual-world networks

Contact types include: advisors, PIs, committee members, mentors, collaborators, letter writers, hiring managers, and conference contacts.

## Core loop

1. **Capture** a contact
2. The card **ages over time** as the relationship goes quiet
3. **AI drafts a follow-up** message
4. **Sending it** resurrects the card back to Glowing

## Card states

Cards move through five states based on time since last contact:

| State | Feel |
|---|---|
| **Glowing** | Freshly connected — warm, bright |
| **Warm** | Recently touched — still good |
| **Cooling** | Going quiet — gentle nudge territory |
| **Smoldering** | Fading fast — needs attention |
| **Ash** | Moved to **The Ashes** — dormant archive |

Logging any interaction (email, coffee, conference wave) rekindles the card back to Glowing.

## Tone and voice

- Warm, a little spooky, never guilt-trippy
- Encouraging — like a good mentor, not a productivity app
- The product feels like it cares about you, not your metrics
- Avoid language that frames neglected contacts as failures; frame them as opportunities

## Key UX metaphors

- The **spinning card file** (Rolodex) is the central visual — physical, tactile, nostalgic
- Cards visually degrade over time (color, texture, glow) to show relationship health
- **The Ashes** is the graveyard for fully dormant contacts — haunted but retrievable
- Fire and ember metaphors run throughout: glow, warmth, cooling, smoldering, spark, rekindle
